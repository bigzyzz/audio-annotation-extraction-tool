# Appendix B: Risk Register

Living register. `project_plan.pdf` Appendix B is the submitted snapshot — this file is the source of truth for all project and technical risks throughout development.

> [!NOTE]
> Risks are mapped to system requirements (R1–R9) where applicable. Living IDs use `RK*` to distinguish risk entries from requirement IDs (`R1`–`R9`), while cross-referencing original Appendix B numbering.

## How to use

- **Sprint Reviews**: Audit Open and High-score risks during the weekly sprint review (5 min) per project plan §4.4.1.
- **Risk Formulation**: Write new risks following the format: _Risk of [event] caused by [root cause] resulting in [consequence]_.
- **Score Calculation**: `Score = Probability (%) × Impact (1–10 scale)`.
- **Status Lifecycle**: `Not monitored - to be implemented` · `Monitored` · `Triggered` · `Mitigated` · `Accepted` · `Closed`.
- **Response Strategy**: When a trigger condition is met, the Risk Owner executes the **Reduction** strategy. If milestone delivery is threatened, deploy the **Fallback** plan.
